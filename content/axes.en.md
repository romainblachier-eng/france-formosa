## Intellectual and academic production

France Formosa contributes to public debate through publications in leading journals such as **Revue Conflits**, the **Taipei Times** and **Taiwan Insight**. Our members regularly speak at higher education institutions and at conferences dedicated to Taiwanese geopolitics.

Our ambition is to foster quality reflection on Franco-Taiwanese issues and to promote French expertise on Taiwan.

---

## Field diplomacy

The association has led **two official missions under the auspices of Taiwan's MOFA** (Ministry of Foreign Affairs) in **2019** and **2025**, reflecting the institutional recognition of our work.

We maintain ongoing relations with:
- The **Taipei Representative Office in France**
- The **Taiwan Office in Aix-en-Provence**
- **MOFA Foreign Media Services**

These connections allow us to serve as a facilitator in bilateral exchanges.

---

## Cultural mediation and events

France Formosa develops conference projects, event partnerships and initiatives aimed at **raising Taiwan's visibility in France** and fostering intercultural exchanges.

We organise and participate in events that highlight the richness of Taiwanese culture for French audiences.

---

## Institutional cooperation

We have initiated outreach to **Taiwan's Ministry of Culture (MOC)** to build lasting partnerships in the fields of cultural mediation, events and exchanges between the two countries.

This approach reflects our commitment to building solid institutional bridges between France and Taiwan.
