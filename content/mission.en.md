## A clear purpose

France Formosa is a Franco-Taiwanese cultural diplomacy association. Its mission is to enrich the cultural and intellectual ties between France and Taiwan by combining academic research, institutional relations and concrete cultural projects.

Founded on the conviction that strong relationships are also built through culture, research and civic dialogue, France Formosa acts as a bridge between French and Taiwanese civil societies.

## Our founding principles

### Intellectual independence
Our analyses and publications are guided by academic rigour and objectivity, free from partisan affiliation.

### Intercultural dialogue
We believe in the richness of exchanges between cultures and civilisations, and in the power of dialogue to bring peoples closer together.

### Shared commitment
France and Taiwan share strong bonds rooted in common values that we are dedicated to deepening and promoting.

### Concrete action
Beyond reflection, we carry out fieldwork: dialogue missions, conferences, cultural events and institutional partnerships.
